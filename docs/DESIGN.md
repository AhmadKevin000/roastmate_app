## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a warm technical minimalism aesthetic, replacing sterile laboratory software conventions with an artisanal, grounded coffee-grading atmosphere. It strikes an intentional equilibrium between computational precision (computer vision defect classification, extraction metrics, indicative tier grading) and human sensory craft (roasting profiles, tactile surfaces, ritualistic manual brewing).

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Artisanal Rigor:** Reassuring, analytical, and scientifically sound without feeling clinical or intimidating.
- **Warm & Organic:** Earthy undertones, linen-like surface hues, and rich roast-derived pigments evoke roasted seeds, unglazed ceramic drippers, and natural coffee parchment.
- **Transparent & Direct:** Clear distinction between algorithmic guidance and certified physical appraisal, prioritizing contextual disclosure ("Summary → evidence → detail → action").

### Visual Philosophy
Rooted in Material 3 foundations with tactile restraint, the design system avoids pure grays, harsh chromatic synthetics, and decorative clutter. Form follows functional clarity: precise data grids, clear information architecture, high-contrast legibility, and physical semantic indicators that pair every numerical score or color tag with clear contextual iconography and explanatory text.

## Colors

The color system builds upon calibrated roast chemistry, botanical greens, and unbleached parchment tones.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#5A382C` - Espresso):** The core anchor for prominent calls-to-action, major navigation focus, and core brand recognition.
- **Secondary (`#8B5E3C` - Terracotta):** Represents warm clay, supporting supplementary functional actions and highlighted sensory variables.
- **Tertiary (`#5E6B4A` - Sage):** Fresh, plant-based botanical green used for positive validation, optimal brewing milestones, and clean defect checks.
- **Neutral (`#796E66` - Warm Umber):** Organic neutral foundation that informs borders, dividers, secondary text, and inactive iconography.

### Semantic Tiers and Defect Mapping
Color is never used in isolation for status communication. All defect indicators and quality grades must bundle a minimum 4.5:1 text contrast ratio, clear icon indicators, and unambiguous text copy:
- **Defects:** Healthy (`#5E6B4A`), Broken (`#9B6A3D`), Insect Damage (`#B54A43`), Quaker (`#C49038`), Scorched (`#6B3F34`), Foreign Matter (`#6D5A86`).
- **Indicative Grades:** Specialty (`#3F6B4A`), Premium (`#496574`), Standard (`#8B5E00`), Below Standard (`#A85E32`), Off Grade (`#B3261E`).

### Quality Profile → Indicative Grade Mapping
Quality Profile kanonik adalah `High` · `Medium` · `Low`. Tampilan grade dipetakan sebagai berikut (parameter sistem, dapat dikonfigurasi):

| Quality Profile | Score | Indicative Grade |
|---|---|---|
| High | 85–100 | Specialty |
| High | 75–84 | Premium |
| Medium | 60–74 | Standard |
| Low | 40–59 | Below Standard |
| Low | 0–39 | Off Grade |

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes **Inter** across all display, body, and label roles to maintain structural precision, neutral neutrality, and extreme legibility when parsing complex data tables and machine vision logs.

### Application Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for indicative overall quality scores, extraction percentages, and top-level screen names. Set with a tight tracking rhythm and firm weights (700) to project structural stability.
- **Titles & Subtitles:** Standardized to 600 weight to maintain scan-reading efficiency across dense recipe steps and defect counters without creating typographic vibration.
- **Body:** Neutral weights (400) optimized for sustained reading during brewing guides, tasting notes, and diagnostic explanations.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Set with slight positive letter-spacing (`0.1px` to `0.3px`) and semi-bold weights (600) to ensure immediate decipherability in all-caps indicative grade tags (`GRADE INDIKATIF — SPECIALTY`) and compact metric pills.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows a disciplined 4 dp base coordinate grid with an 8 dp primary structural step.

### Spacing Scale & Usage
- **`space-xs` (4px / 0.25rem):** Micro padding within chips, between closely tied badges, and inner button edge alignments.
- **`space-sm` (8px / 0.5rem):** Standard spacing between icons and text labels; tight grid gaps between tag collections.
- **`space-md` (12px / 0.75rem):** Vertical spacing between card internal content groups; compact list item separations.
- **`space-lg` (16px / 1rem):** Default interior card padding and compact mobile margin padding.
- **`space-xl` (24px / 1.5rem):** Vertical gaps between distinct feature modules, major section headings, and bottom sheet containers.

### Responsive Grids & Adaptation
- **Compact Handset (< 600dp):** Single-column presentation. Margins are fixed at `1rem` (16dp). Cards span full width. Floating thumb actions enforce an absolute minimal bounding box of 48×48dp.
- **Medium Screens (600–839dp):** Dynamic 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters. Metric overview modules split into 2 to 3 column card blocks. Max content width is centered at 720dp.
- **Expanded Screens (≥ 840dp):** Centered 12-column layout capped at 840dp to prevent wide line lengths on brewing guides and preserve visual connection between defect bounding boxes and score sheets.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system embraces a "low elevation" philosophy, favoring warm tonal layering over deep, artificial shadows. Spatial hierarchy is defined by distinct surface container values that gently lift elements against the unbleached `#F7F3EE` canvas.

### Surface Tiers
- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F7F3EE` — The warm background that grounds the entire interface.
- **Layer 1 (Resting Cards & Modules):** `#FFFDF9` — Pure tinted off-white used for primary cards, grading summaries, and inspection list tiles. Features no harsh drop shadow, relying instead on a subtle outline of `#D9CDC4` or a low-intensity ambient diffusion (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(43, 36, 33, 0.05)`).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive & Selected Cards):** `#FBF7F2` to `#EDE4DD` — Active selection containers, hover states, and drag states.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Persistent Bottom Sheets):** `#FFFDF9` elevated with an ambient tint shadow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(43, 36, 33, 0.12)`).
- **Inverse / High-Focus Overlay:** `#2B2421` — Used for camera utility overlays, dark bottom toasts, and active flash/guide controls, creating an authoritative visual break from daytime inspection modes.

## Shapes

The design system uses a rounded corner geometry (`roundedness: 2`) that balances technical precision with organic tactile forms, reminiscent of rounded ceramic wares and smoothed coffee beans.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Micro (`8px`):** Status indicators, inline metric pills, micro chips, and dense technical tags.
- **Small (`12px`):** Outlined input fields, camera detection overlay frames, and compact sub-cards.
- **Medium (`16px`):** Standard default cards (`StatCard`, `RecipeCard`, `ScanCtaCard`).
- **Large (`24px`):** High-priority hero containers, summary scorecards, and top corners of bottom modal sheets.
- **Maximum / Full (`999px`):** Defect chip filters, active buttons, and the circular primary camera capture trigger.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Buttons:** Solid fill with `brand.espresso` (`#5A382C`), label text in `#FFFDF9` (weight: 600), radius of `12px` or `999px` (depending on prominence), minimum height of `48px`. On pressed states, surface transitions to `brand.espressoDark` (`#3D251D`).
- **Secondary Buttons:** Outlined border of `1.5px` using `brand.terracotta` (`#8B5E3C`) or filled container using `brand.terracottaSoft` (`#F2E2D5`) with terracotta text for supporting actions.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Buttons:** Transparent fill, `48px` minimum touch target, text set in `text.primary` (`#211A17`) with active underline or tonal container highlight on hover/tap.

### Chips & Badges
- **Defect Chips:** Pill-shaped (`radius.full`), padding `4px 12px`, featuring an icon, short defect title, and frequency count (e.g., `[!] Quaker (3)`). Background set to corresponding container color (e.g., `#F8E5B5` for quaker) with strong contrasting label (`#8B5E00`).
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive state uses `surface.container` (`#F2ECE6`) with `border.default` (`#D9CDC4`). Active state uses `brand.espressoSoft` (`#F0DED4`) with an active stroke and bold `brand.espresso` text.

### Cards
- **Base Structure:** Built using `surface.primary` (`#FFFDF9`) with corner radius of `16px`. Separation achieved via either a 1px border (`border.default`) or a subtle tonal container step (`surface.containerLow`).
- **Inspection Detail Cards:** Grouped bean metrics containing tabular breakdowns of color uniformity and size distribution grids.

### Input Fields
- **Text Inputs:** Outlined frame (`radius.sm` / 12px), background `#FFFDF9`, stroke `1px` in `border.default` (`#D9CDC4`).
- **Active Focus:** Border transitions to `2px` in `border.strong` (`#806F65`) or `brand.espresso` (`#5A382C`) with no synthetic outer glow. Label floats upward maintaining `Inter` 12px regular.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Selection States:** Unselected control displays a `2px` stroke in `border.strong` (`#806F65`). Selected state fills with `brand.espresso` (`#5A382C`) displaying a crisp checkmark or solid center dot in `#FFFDF9`. Target area strictly constrained to `≥ 48 × 48 dp`.

### Lists
- **Layout:** Separated by hairline divider `border.default` (`#D9CDC4`). Each item features an icon or preview thumbnail on the leading edge (40×40dp, `radius.sm`), primary label in `text.primary`, subtitle in `text.secondary`, and trailing metadata or chevron.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Indicative Grade Banner:** Structured hero container displaying grade status with a colored left-border accent (4px stroke in the grade's primary hex), grade name in full caps (`GRADE INDIKATIF — SPECIALTY`), alongside an information disclosure icon reminding users of the screening-grade nature of optical classification.
- **Brewing Step Guide:** Numbered step cards for the Detail Resep page (preparation, blooming, pours, serving). Each step shows a title, description, and a **static target-time label** (e.g., `00:00–00:45`) as reference text. This is a static guide — it contains **no active timer, countdown, or start/stop control**.
